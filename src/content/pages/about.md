Second Ink is where I put a note when something I’ve read is still on my mind. Writing it down helps me see what changed in my thinking.

I come back to a few questions about making things. When is a draft ready for another reader? Which bit of friction is worth fixing? Who makes the decisions tucked into an everyday tool?

When you have a moment at the end of an essay, tell me how it landed. I get to see your note. Other readers see only how the responses add up.

The sample reflections are published under the placeholder pen name Mara Ellis.
