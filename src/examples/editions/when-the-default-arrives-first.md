Some defaults are worth borrowing. I like the ones that get an ordinary job out of the way, and I get curious when a tool seems to have picked the question before I’ve found mine. I put these essays in the order I’d use at my desk. Notice the suggestion, look at what happens when you follow it, then try a smaller beginning of your own.
